
# Executive Summary

Afficionado Coffee Roasters faces highly dynamic customer demand patterns across different locations and hours. This project implements a data-driven forecasting framework to predict future revenue trends, identify peak demand periods, and support intelligent operational planning.

The Streamlit dashboard provides:
- Store-wise forecasting
- Demand visualization
- KPI monitoring
- Peak-hour prediction
- Forecast accuracy metrics

Business Benefits:
- Reduced inventory waste
- Better staffing schedules
- Improved customer satisfaction
- Data-driven planning decisions
